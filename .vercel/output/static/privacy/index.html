Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 May 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Cape Town Roofing Co. collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through our website. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

By using our website or submitting an enquiry, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

Who We Are

Cape Town Roofing Co. is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website. If you have any questions about how we handle your information, please contact us:

Email: info@capetownroofing.co.za

Phone: +27 21 555 0100

Address: 12 Long Street

What Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily submit an enquiry through our contact form. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • A message describing your enquiry or the service you are interested in

We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by POPIA. We do not collect any information from visitors who do not submit an enquiry, other than the basic technical data processed automatically by our website hosting provider (see Third-Party Services below).

Why We Collect Your Information

We collect and process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss our services with you
  • To contact you by phone or email as requested
  • To keep a record of our communications with you
  • To send you a confirmation that your enquiry was received
  • To send one follow-up message if we have not yet connected with you

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, and we do not add you to mailing lists without your explicit consent.

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your information is stored securely using reputable third-party services listed in the section below. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, loss, disclosure, or misuse.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose your personal information to any third party for their own purposes.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your enquiry information for as long as is reasonably necessary to manage our business relationship with you — typically up to three years from the date of your last contact with us. After this period, your information is securely deleted.

You may request deletion of your information at any time (see Your Rights below).

Third-Party Services

To operate our website and respond to your enquiry, we use the following third-party services. Each service processes information under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

Google reCAPTCHA

Our contact form is protected by Google reCAPTCHA to prevent automated spam submissions. reCAPTCHA may process your IP address, browser information, and interaction patterns. This data is processed by Google under their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Form Processing Service

When you submit an enquiry, your information is processed by an automation service that delivers your message to us and logs it in a secure record. This service acts as a data processor on our behalf and does not use your information for its own purposes.

Email Delivery Service

We use a third-party email delivery service to send you a confirmation when you submit an enquiry, and to deliver our response to your email address. This service processes your email address solely to deliver these messages.

Website Hosting Provider

Our website is hosted on a cloud platform that processes basic server log data — such as IP addresses and page requests — as part of normal website operations. This data is used only for security and performance purposes and is not linked to any form submission you make.

Your Rights Under POPIA

The Protection of Personal Information Act gives you the following rights in relation to your personal information:

  • Right to access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You may ask us to correct any information that is inaccurate or outdated.
  • Right to deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to any legal obligations that require us to retain it.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@capetownroofing.co.za. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time.

Information Regulator of South Africa

If you are not satisfied with our response to any complaint or concern about the handling of your personal information, you may contact the Information Regulator of South Africa:

Website: www.inforegulator.org.za

Email: inforeg@justice.gov.za

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
