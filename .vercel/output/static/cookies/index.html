What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly and to provide website owners with basic information about how their sites are being used.

Cookies do not give us access to your device and cannot be used to run programs or deliver viruses to your computer.

How We Use Cookies

Cape Town Roofing Co. uses cookies to:

  • Ensure our website and contact form function correctly
  • Protect our contact form from automated spam submissions

We do not use cookies to identify you personally, and we do not use cookies to serve you targeted advertising.

Managing Your Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, or notify you when a cookie is being set. Instructions for common browsers are linked below:
